Araceae

The arum family — leaves that shelter a spadix and spathe.

Genus: Monstera

No. 0142
Swiss cheese plant
Monstera deliciosa
Family: Araceae Genus: Monstera Species: deliciosa
📍 Native: Southern Mexico to Panama

Wide, glossy leaves that develop fenestrations as the plant matures. Climbs by aerial roots in its native range.

No. 0143
Shingle plant
Monstera acuminata
Family: Araceae Genus: Monstera Species: acuminata
📍 Native: Central America & Mexico

A tropical climbing vine native to Central America and Mexico, famous for its unique "shingle" growth habit and holey leaves.

Genus: Spathiphyllum

No. 0144
Peace lily
Spathiphyllum wallisii
Family: Araceae Genus: Spathiphyllum Species: wallisii
📍 Native:Venezuela

Dark lance-shaped leaves and a single white spathe curling around a pale spadix. Tolerant of low light.

No. 0145
Canna-leaf peace lily
Spathiphyllum cannifolium
Family: Araceae Genus: Spathiphyllum Species: cannifolium
📍 Native: Northern South America

A tropical, evergreen herbaceous perennial belonging to the Araceae (arum) family.

Rosaceae

The rose family — five-petalled flowers, often with thorns.

Genus: Rosa

No. 0091
Dog rose
Rosa canina
Family: Rosaceae Genus: Rosa Species: canina
📍 Native: Europe & Western Asia

A scrambling hedgerow rose with pale pink flowers and hooked thorns along an arching stem.

No. 0092
Lady Banks' rose
Rosa banksiae
Family: Rosaceae Genus: Rosa Species: banksiae
📍 Native: China

A fast-growing, nearly thornless climbing shrub native to central and western China.

Genus: Fragaria

No. 0093
Wild strawberry
Fragaria vesca
Family: Rosaceae Genus: Fragaria Species: vesca
📍 Native:North America

Trifoliate leaves and small white flowers, followed by fruit far smaller than its cultivated relatives.

No. 0094
Musk strawberry
Fragaria moschata
Family: Rosaceae Genus: Fragaria Species: moschata
📍 Native: Europe

A European wild strawberry species prized for its intensely aromatic, uniquely flavored fruit.

Fabaceae

The legume family — pod fruits and nitrogen-fixing roots.

Genus: Lathyrus

No. 0210
Garden pea
Lathyrus oleraceus
Family: Fabaceae Genus: Lathyrus Species: oleraceus
📍 Native: Europe

The cultivated garden pea, traditionally known as Pisum sativum, is an annual legume grown for its edible seeds and pods.

No. 0211
Tawny pea
Lathyrus fulvus
Family: Fabaceae Genus: Lathyrus Species: fulvus
📍 Native: Middle East

A wild pea native to the eastern Mediterranean; it is traditionally known as Pisum fulvum.

Genus: Trifolium

No. 0212
Red clover
Trifolium pratense
Family: Fabaceae Genus: Trifolium Species: pratense
📍 Native: Europe, western Asia & northwest Africa

A familiar forage legume with three-part leaves and rounded heads of pink-red flowers.

No. 0213
White clover
Trifolium repens
Family: Fabaceae Genus: Trifolium Species: repens
📍 Native: Europe & western Asia

A low-growing perennial clover with creeping stems, three-part leaves and rounded heads of white flowers.

Asparagaceae

A family of strap-like leaves, redrawn by taxonomists more than once.

Genus: Dracaena

No. 0304
Snake plant
Dracaena trifasciata
Family: Asparagaceae Genus: Dracaena Species: trifasciata
📍 Native: Central Africa

Stiff, upright leaves banded in mottled green. Reclassified from Sansevieria in recent revisions.

No. 0305
Dragon blood tree
Dracaena cinnabari
Family: Asparagaceae Genus: Dracaena Species: cinnabari
📍 Native: Socotra Archipelago

A rare, iconic evergreen tree famous for its dense, umbrella-shaped canopy and crimson-red resin.

Genus: Chlorophytum

No. 0306
Spider plant
Chlorophytum comosum
Family: Asparagaceae Genus: Chlorophytum Species: comosum
📍 Native: Southern Africa

Arching grass-like leaves that send out long runners tipped with plantlets.

No. 0307
Musli
Chlorophytum tuberosum
Family: Asparagaceae Genus: Chlorophytum Species: tuberosum
📍 Native: Circar mountains, India

A small, tuberous perennial herb in the asparagus family that grows 20 to 50 cm tall.
