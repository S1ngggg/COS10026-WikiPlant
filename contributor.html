A shared field guide

Science grows through shared observations

WikiPlant uses a citizen science approach to invite people to document plants they encounter in forests, parks, gardens and other landscapes.

A clear photograph paired with a date, place and habitat note gives researchers useful context for reviewing an observation.

Respect the plant and its habitat. Photograph plants in place, avoid collecting or damaging them, and do not share precise locations for sensitive species.

A useful record

How contributions help

Good observations are specific, respectful and easy for another person to review. Before submitting a record:

  1. 01
    Observe without disturbance

    Leave the plant and its habitat as you found them.

  2. 02
    Photograph useful details

    Capture the whole plant, then close views of leaves, flowers or fruit.

  3. 03
    Add field context

    Record the date, a suitable location and the habitat where it was growing.

  4. 04
    Review before submitting

    Check that your information is accurate and your photograph is clear before submitting the observation.

Before you submit

  • Use clear, relevant photographs.
  • Include the plant name if known.
  • Describe the habitat and observation date.
  • Do not disturb the plant or its surroundings.
