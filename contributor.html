Community Contributions

About Community Contributions

WikiPlant uses a citizen science approach to encourage members of the public to contribute useful observations about Malaysian plant biodiversity. Community members can share plant photographs, plant names and observations that may help researchers and other users understand the plants found in different environments.

Contributions can provide valuable information about plant occurrences in forests, parks, gardens and other natural areas. By bringing together observations from different contributors, WikiPlant can support the collection of plant information on a larger scale. Contributors are encouraged to provide clear photographs and accurate information while respecting the natural environment where the plant is observed.

Users who would like to contribute their own plant observations can use the contribution form. The submitted information is intended to support the plant biodiversity monitoring activities represented by the WikiPlant project.

How Contributions Help

Community observations can support biodiversity monitoring by increasing the amount of plant information available to the WikiPlant project. Contributors can help by following a few simple steps when recording an observation.

  1. Observe the plant without damaging or disturbing it.
  2. Take a clear photograph of the plant.
  3. Record the plant name and relevant observation details.
  4. Submit the information through the contribution form.

Plant Information Terms

Family
A taxonomic group containing related genera that share important biological characteristics.
Genus
A classification group containing one or more closely related plant species.
Species
A specific group of plants that share defining biological characteristics and can be identified as a particular type.
Citizen Science
Scientific research that involves members of the public contributing observations or information to support research activities.

Quick Guidelines

  • Provide accurate plant information.
  • Upload clear and relevant photographs.
  • Record the observation location when possible.
  • Do not damage or disturb plants.

Before You Contribute

Make sure your photograph is clear and that the information you provide is based on your observation. Contributions should support biodiversity monitoring without disturbing the natural environment.
