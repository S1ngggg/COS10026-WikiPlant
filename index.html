The WikiPlant story

A Small observations,
makes a difference.

Imagine walking by a familiar path., yet dozens of flowers you never take notice of. The natural world has a way of inviting us to look a little closer.

WikiPlant brings those moments together. Our open botanical catalog connects plant enthusiasts, students, gardeners, or you name it, through a shared curiosity about Malaysia's plant life!

Browse the relationships between plants, learn what makes each one different, and contribute observations from your own surroundings. Each record is a small step towards a richer picture of the biodiversity we share.
