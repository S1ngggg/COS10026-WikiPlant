Here, you'll discover the enhancements we've made to our website.

Fluid Layout (Hamburger Menu)

The website uses a responsive fluid layout that adapts to different screen sizes. On smaller screens, the navigation links are replaced by a hamburger menu, allowing users to access the navigation without taking up excessive screen space.

Fixed Navigation Bar

Navigation bar remains accessible while users browse the page.

A fixed navigation design keeps the main navigation easily accessible while users move through the website. This reduces the need to repeatedly return to the top of a page when users want to visit another section of WikiPlant.

Plant image hover effect

Picture hover effects provide visual feedback when users move the pointer over plant images. The effect improves interactivity and helps users recognise that an image or plant card can provide additional information.

Flip Card

CSS-only flip card

CSS-only flip cards are used to present plant information in two sides. The front can display a plant image while the back can provide additional information such as the plant name, scientific name or description. This improves information presentation without requiring JavaScript.
